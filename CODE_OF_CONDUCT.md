# Code of conduct

English is the authoritative version; a Chinese translation follows.

MoriMeta is a small project made for photographers. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space — is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, including with people who are new to photography metadata, to software, or to English;
- discuss the work, not the person; disagree with reasons;
- keep other people's photos and personal data private: never post someone else's files, locations, serial numbers or names without their consent;
- not harass, insult, threaten or exclude anyone, for any reason, and not post sexual or violent content.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may block people who do not follow this, for as long as needed. Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub (the repository owner's profile), or through private vulnerability reporting when it concerns security. Reports are kept confidential.

# 行为准则

以英文版为准。

MoriMeta 是一个为摄影师制作的小型项目。所有参与者——无论是在 Issue、Pull Request、讨论还是其他项目空间中——都应当：

- 尊重他人、保持耐心，包括对刚接触照片元数据、软件或英语的人；
- 讨论工作本身，而不是针对个人；有分歧时给出理由；
- 保护他人的照片和个人信息：未经同意，不得公开他人的文件、位置、序列号或姓名；
- 不以任何理由骚扰、侮辱、威胁或排斥任何人，不发布色情或暴力内容。

对于不遵守本准则的人，维护者可以编辑或删除其贡献，并可在必要的时间内屏蔽其参与。如遇问题，请通过 GitHub 私下联系维护者（仓库所有者的主页）；涉及安全问题时请使用私有漏洞报告。所有报告都会保密处理。
